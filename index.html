Proficiency model

Skills and saves: add the linked ability modifier to proficiency (untrained +0; trained/expert/master/legendary add level +2/+4/+6/+8). Saves use Constitution for Fortitude, Dexterity for Reflex, and Wisdom for Will. Legendary unlocks at level 9, the maximum level.

        Status effects

        The armor rating, the game's 50% blast resistance for explosions, and armor passive and booster resistances for the chosen damage type are applied first (listed under the diver). The selected body part loses the resulting damage; Main HP loses that damage after its location factor, rounded to the nearest HP. Arms and legs become Broken at 0 HP. A Stim fully restores HP and turns Broken limbs to Injured. Short Rest restores half of missing HP to Main and unbroken parts, while Long Rest restores all HP and advances broken or injured limbs by one recovery stage.

        Attacks

        Attack modifier = ability (Dexterity for guns and throwables, Strength for melee and unarmed) + proficiency in the weapon's category. Categories follow the weapon type: Simple for assault rifles, SMGs, pistols and standard grenades; Martial for shotguns, marksman rifles, energy weapons, melee and special throwables; Advanced for explosive and special guns. Support weapons in your stratagem slots get their own card: Advanced (Martial and Strength for melee tools like the Breaching Hammer), with ammo once called in.

        Stratagems

        Game times are converted to rounds of 6 seconds (3 actions), rounded up. Press Next round at the end of each round to count cooldowns, call-ins and Eagle rearming down. Eagle uses refill when Eagle Rearm finishes; Orbital Laser and Exosuit uses are per mission. Every diver has a fully upgraded Super Destroyer: cooldowns, call-ins, Eagle uses and rearm use the wiki's fully upgraded values; support weapons arrive with all spare magazines, and supply boxes refill them completely. Stratagem fire damage +25%, sentry +50% ammo and +50% health, and larger orbital blasts apply to things this sheet doesn't track.
