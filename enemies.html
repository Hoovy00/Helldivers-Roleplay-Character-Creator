How the numbers are set (house rule, balanced against this table's player math). At Moderate threat: AC = 15 + level (a trained diver of that level hits about half the time), adjusted by body armor (Unarmored −1, Light ±0, Medium +1, Heavy +2, Anti-Tank +3). Armor values (AV) use the same scale as weapon penetration (AP), so a Heavy (AV 4) part needs Heavy (AP 4) penetration; Attack = level + 6; Fort, Ref, Will and Perception = level + 4; Ability DC = level + 15. High threat is +2 to all of these, Low −2. Damage, health and armor are the game's own numbers.

Enemy levels follow the Character builder's level guide (set in data/enemy-levels.js, edit freely): 0–1 fodder and conscripts (Scavenger, Voteless, Trooper), 2–3 veteran soldiers and elites (Hive Guard, Devastator, Overseer), 4–5 specialists (Heavy Devastator, Stalker, Alpha Commander), 6–8 heroic monsters (Charger 6, Hulks 6–7, Impaler and tanks 8), 9 legends (Bile Titan, Dragonroach), and 10+ colossal threats beyond any one diver (Factory Strider and Vox Engine 10, Leviathan 11, Hive Lord 12).

Difficulty (PF2e encounter budget). Each enemy is worth XP by its level compared with the party's: −4: 10 · −3: 15 · −2: 20 · −1: 30 · same: 40 · +1: 60 · +2: 80 · +3: 120 · +4: 160. High threat counts as one level higher, Low as one lower (like PF2e elite and weak). For 4 divers: Trivial 40 · Low 60 · Moderate 80 · Severe 120 · Extreme 160; each diver more or fewer adds or removes 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40. Helldivers throws big hordes of weak enemies, so expect swarms to rate high on paper: treat it as a guide, not a rule.

Hit locations and penetration (wiki Damage page). Pick where the shot lands, the weapon (or type damage and AP by hand) and how many hits. Penetration above the part's armor value deals full damage, equal deals 65%, below ricochets for nothing. The part loses the damage and passes its "% to Main" on to Main HP (capped at the part's full HP); parts listed as "Main" only damage Main. Destroying a fatal part kills the enemy. Explosive damage is cut by the enemy's explosive resistance. The quick damage boxes on compact cards and in the tracker skip all of this and go straight to Main HP.

Initiative. Divers use their sheet's initiative; enemies roll Perception. Type in each roll (from your dice bot), then Sort. On a tie, enemies act first (PF2e). Next turn skips downed enemies and counts rounds; tick the box to count squad stratagem cooldowns down in the Character builder too.
